1 Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Debt Tracker: Personal Ledger ("the Application", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Akuma Productions.

We are deeply committed to protecting your personal privacy. We believe that personal financial tracking—such as tracking loans, IOUs, personal debts, settlements, and shared expenses—is inherently sensitive. Because of this, we designed the Application on a local-first architectural paradigm.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Application handles, how the data is stored, what third-party SDKs are integrated into the Application, what device permissions are requested, and your absolute rights concerning your information under applicable data protection regulations and the Google Play Developer Policy.

2 Personal Data & Financial Records

The Application functions as a self-contained personal ledger. When using the App, you may enter information including:

  • Amounts borrowed or lent, including currency denominations
  • Names, labels, or contact tags of counterparties
  • Transaction dates, due dates, and settlement timestamps
  • Personal notes, descriptions, or transaction history records
Strict Local Storage Declaration

All personal ledger records, financial quantities, names, and notes entered into Debt Tracker: Personal Ledger are stored strictly, exclusively, and permanently on your physical device (utilizing local sandboxed database storage such as SQLite or local key-value stores).

Neither the developer nor any remote server managed by us receives, reads, syncs, backs up, logs, or transmits your ledger entries. We do not operate external application servers holding your financial ledger, and we do not maintain accounts, passwords, or cloud database replicas. Your financial records remain solely in your custody.

5 Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Under modern data protection regulations (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Google Play Data Safety standards), users maintain the right to access, rectify, and permanently erase their information.

Because We Do Not Store Your Data on Servers:

Because all personal ledger records reside exclusively on your physical mobile device, Akuma Productions does not retain, hold, or have access to any copy of your records on external servers. Consequently, we cannot provide an automated web portal or manual server-side deletion request, because there is literally nothing stored on our side to delete.

How You Retain Full Control Over Your Data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or permanently delete individual debt records, settlements, and notes at any time using the delete options within the App.
  • Clearing App Data: You can instantly wipe 100% of your records and reset the application to its original clean state through Android System Settings:
    Settings > Apps > Debt Tracker: Personal Ledger > Storage & cache > Clear Storage (Clear Data).
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Debt Tracker: Personal Ledger from your device permanently purges the local database and all associated ledger records from your hardware.
